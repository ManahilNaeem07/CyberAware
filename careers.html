Cybersecurity Careers

Explore 15 key cybersecurity roles and what each professional does.

1. Security Operations Center (SOC) Analyst

Monitors organizational networks continuously for security alerts, investigates suspicious activity, and escalates potential cyber incidents.

2. Penetration Tester (Ethical Hacker)

Conducts authorized simulated cyberattacks on systems, networks, and applications to find and report exploitable vulnerabilities.

3. Incident Responder

Acts as a first responder during active breaches or ransomware attacks to contain threats, minimize damage, and restore affected systems.

4. Security Engineer

Designs, builds, and maintains security infrastructure including firewalls, intrusion detection systems, and automated defense tools.

5. Security Architect

Creates comprehensive, enterprise-wide security blueprints and system designs to protect an organization's technology infrastructure.

6. Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)

Leads the entire cybersecurity department at the executive level, overseeing security strategy, budgets, governance, and risk management.

7. Digital Forensics Analyst

Collects, preserves, and analyzes digital evidence from compromised devices and networks to investigate cybercrimes and support legal cases.

8. Malware Analyst

Examines and reverse-engineers malicious software such as viruses, trojans, and ransomware to understand how they work and how to stop them.

9. Cyber Threat Intelligence Analyst

Researches and analyzes emerging threat actors, attack trends, and vulnerabilities to help organizations anticipate and prevent future attacks.

10. Cloud Security Engineer

Secures cloud-based infrastructure, platforms, and data storage by configuring access controls, encryption, and cloud compliance rules.

11. Application Security Engineer

Integrates security controls into the software development lifecycle and audits source code to prevent flaws in web and mobile apps.

12. Identity and Access Management (IAM) Engineer

Manages user authentication systems, permissions, and single sign-on tools to ensure only authorized people access sensitive resources.

13. Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) Analyst

Ensures the organization follows data privacy laws, industry regulations, and internal security policies while assessing operational risks.

14. Information Security Auditor

Evaluates and tests an organization's IT systems, controls, and procedures to verify security effectiveness and regulatory compliance.

15. Cryptography Engineer

Develops and implements encryption algorithms, ciphers, and public key infrastructure to protect sensitive data during storage and transmission.
